## Zenkora's Dark Moon - Main Page
The personal internet corner of a 30-year-old dude with dusty dreams.

Personal websites and blogs are all but dead, yet this one persists. It's moved
across domains, servers, and states, been accidentally destroyed and remade a
handful of times, but I still hold onto the delusion that a morsel of the tiny
minority of web traffic not going to a MAANG service might turn into eyeballs
on my words. Or, far more likely, some Generation-Tau YTer is gonna scroll
through here in 2095 after I'm long dead, and make goofy exaggerated reactions
for a video about the "ancient" and "crusty" 2020s internet.
