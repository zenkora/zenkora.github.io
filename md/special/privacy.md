## Elden Cloud - Privacy
No information about you is stored by this site. But the following third-party
services are used, and *they* might store data about you.

### Facebook
The comments sections around this site are operated by Facebook. You know how
much data Meta collects and what they do with it. Yet we all use their shit.

### country.is
This site's frontend JS does country-level IP geolocation. I don't think that's
terribly invasive.

### Google
This site uses Google Analytics and AdSense. These services are taken out by
pretty much any ad blocker, and it won't hurt my feelings if you use one. But
I'd like to make some pennies from unlearned, non-adblock-using scrubs if
possible, and to at least know some things about my site visitors.
