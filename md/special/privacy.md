## Elden Cloud - Privacy
No information about you is stored by this extremely minimal website. But the
following third-party services are used, and *they* might store data about you.
Feel free to look up their privacy policies.

### Facebook
The comments sections around this site are operated by Facebook. You know how
much data Meta collects and what they do with it. Yet we all use their shit.

### country.is
This site's frontend JS does country-level IP geolocation. I don't think that's
terribly invasive.

### Google
This site uses Google Analytics and AdSense. These services are taken out by
pretty much any ad blocker, and it won't hurt my feelings if you use one.
Because I sure as hell do too. But I'd like to make some pennies from
unlearned, non-adblock-using scrubs if possible, and to at least know some
things about my site visitors.
